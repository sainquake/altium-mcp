Only place pins on the left and right side of the symbol. Do not combine or overlap pins. In order of most important to least: ground pins in the bottom left (analog, digital, power, any ground pins), no connect pins in the bottom right, place power rail pins at the upper right, inputs on the left, outputs on the right and for sw regulators vin on the left. Try to group other pins together by similar functionality (for example, SPI, I2C, RGMII, etc.).
Always separate groups by 100mil gaps unless there is extra spacing, then space out groups equal distance from each other.
Left side and Bottom denote lower values of x & y. Before generating the symbol provide an explanation for why you chose each pins position.
Every new symbol must be created with nonempty Value, MF, MP, HelpURL, Package, Type, Instructions and System parameters. Use actual datasheet-backed data; do not invent missing manufacturer names, part numbers, ratings or URLs. Use Fit for ordinary assembly instructions or DNP when intentionally not fitted, and name the functional block in System. Pacage and Pacage Metric are obsolete; use Package. JLCPCB/LCSC parameters are optional.
Write a searchable native Description stating the component category, function, key specifications and package. Example: Resistor, 10k, 1%, 100mW, 0603 SMD. Do not use a placeholder, the symbol name or an MPN alone. Pass description separately from custom parameters. The creation tool enforces required metadata, sets Comment to MP and hides it, and displays Value below the symbol. Do not add VALUE=VALUE, PLACE or PKG_STYLE placeholders.
